Potential effects and risks of smoking

Overview

Are you a smoker and are curious about the possible risks and effects it might have on your health? Then you have come to the right place! On this webpage, you will be able to access different visualisation techniques that show diseases related to smoking, and their respective risk factors and symptoms. The data used was extracted from WikiData through SPARQL queries.

Network

Explore all the diseases that can be caused by smoking, with all their risk factors and symptoms.

Bar Charts

Explore more quantifiable information on the diseases caused by smoking.

Pie chart

Explore how frequent symptoms are amongst all diseases caused by smoking.
